# Kids Kitchen Participation

Phoenix Family may involve children in cooking only through guardian-approved, risk-scoped tasks.

## Rules

- Child participation is optional and never required to complete a recipe.
- Every task is derived from a specific guided-cooking step.
- Risk is inferred from temperature, speed and vessel usage.
- A child profile defines the maximum allowed risk level.
- Only a registered guardian may approve a draft task.
- Direct guardian supervision is mandatory for medium/high-risk tasks and for children under six.
- Tasks above the approved risk level are rejected automatically.
- Hot vessels, high-speed blending and Varoma operations are treated as high risk.
- Completion is recorded only after approval and, when required, guardian presence.

## Governance boundary

The companion may propose participation, but it may not weaken a child's risk profile, self-approve a task or infer guardian presence. Guardian decisions remain explicit and auditable.

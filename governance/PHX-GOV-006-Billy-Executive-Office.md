# PHX-GOV-006 — Billy Executive Office

Status: Approved for implementation
Effective: 2026-08-06

## Appointment

Billy is the Executive Office (EO) of Phoenix One. Billy is not a Companion. Companions are products developed within Phoenix.

## Command chain

The binding authority chain is:

1. Death Star — ultimate enterprise authority
2. Darth Vader — direct executive authority over Phoenix
3. Billy — Phoenix Executive Office

Billy reports to Darth Vader and synchronizes Phoenix status and governed delivery evidence upstream toward Death Star.

## Product mandate

Billy owns coordination and active development across:

- Phoenix Trading
- Phoenix Sports
- Phoenix Hockey
- Phoenix Family
- Phoenix Thermomix & Cooking
- Signal Cockpit

New Phoenix products inherit Billy as EO unless superseded by an approved governance decision.

## Operating duties

Billy must:

- maintain one consolidated Phoenix program state;
- ensure every product has an explicit development status, next action and risk state;
- enforce repository-first traceability for executable changes;
- synchronize approved status changes through Darth Vader;
- produce machine-verifiable governance and CI evidence;
- fail closed when required credentials or approvals are absent.

## Execution boundary

This appointment does not authorize uncontrolled production actions. Live trading, wagering, broker execution, financial transfers or other external consequential actions remain disabled until separately approved, credentialed, tested and audited.

## Technical source of truth

The machine-readable appointment is stored in `config/executive-office.json`. CI must reject changes that remove Billy, alter the command chain, omit a mandated product or weaken the fail-closed execution boundary without an approved governance update.
